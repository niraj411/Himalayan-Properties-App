The process

Walk the spaceCall or text (510) 388-5124. Showings most days, usually within 24 hours.
Send the short applicationOnline at the link below, about 5 minutes. Business name, contact, intended use, desired term, guarantor name. No documents needed at this step.
Letter of intentWe send a one-page LOI with rent, term, start date and any build-out or free-rent request. You mark it up, we agree on the business terms.
Financials and leaseBefore the LOI is signed we ask for the documents below. Then the lease, on the same form the other tenants in the center signed, followed by deposit and first month's rent at signing.

What to have ready before the LOI

Legal business name and entity type (LLC, corp, sole prop) and state of formation
Owner or principal name, phone, email, home address (the personal guarantor)
Two years of business tax returns (or personal returns for a new business)
Three most recent months of business bank statements
A one-paragraph description of the business and hours of operation
Any prior landlord contact, if you have leased space before
Planned build-out or changes to the space, if any
Desired start date and lease term (3 to 5 years)

At lease signing

Security deposit: two months of base rent plus NNN (about $6,517)
First month's base rent and NNN (about $3,259, prorated if mid-month)
Certificate of insurance: general liability naming Himalayan Holding Property LLC as additional insured
Signed personal guaranty
